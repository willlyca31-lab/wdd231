GoodMind Psychology Network

Site Plan Document

Site Name

GoodMind Psychology Network

The name "GoodMind" represents connection, communication, emotional wellness, and professional collaboration. It reflects the goal of connecting psychologists, mental health professionals, and patients together through a modern digital platform.

Optional domain: GoodMindpsychology.org

Site Purpose

The purpose of this website is to create a professional networking and client connection platform for psychologists and mental health professionals.

The website will allow psychologists to create virtual professional profiles, connect with potential patients, share mental health resources, and collaborate with other professionals.

  • Home page introducing the platform and mission
  • Dynamic psychologist directory generated from JSON data
  • Mental health resources and psychology articles
  • Contact and appointment request forms
  • Interactive modal dialogs for profile details
  • Local storage for user preferences
  • Responsive design for mobile and desktop devices

Scenarios

“How can I find a psychologist who specializes in anxiety and depression?”

“Where can I schedule an appointment with a licensed psychologist online?”

“Where can I read updated mental health articles and professional psychology resources?”
